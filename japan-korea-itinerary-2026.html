Before you book anything

Two document issues are specific to a Brazilian passport in late 2026. Clear these first — they gate the whole trip.

🗤

Japan visa-free status — verify it's still active

The Brazil–Japan reciprocal 90-day visa waiver was signed for an initial term ending Sep 30, 2026. Your trip starts Nov 10, 2026 — after that date.

  • Confirm the waiver was renewed with the Japanese Embassy / MOFA before buying flights. If it lapsed, you'd need a tourist visa.
  • A biometric (electronic) passport is required for visa-free entry.
🇰🇷

Korea needs a K-ETA (Brazil is not exempt)

Brazilians are visa-free for Korea (90 days) but must hold a K-ETA — Brazil is not on the 22-country exemption list.

  • Apply at k-eta.go.kr — ~₩10,000 (≈ US$8), valid 3 years.
  • Processing takes up to 72h — do it at least a week before Nov 23.
Book ~60 days out

🎫 Tokyo Disney tickets

Fixed-date passports open ~2 months ahead (early-Sept 2026). Christmas-season weekends sell out in days. Buy Nov 16 & 17 (Mon/Tue, weekdays).

Sells out 2–3 wks ahead

🌌 teamLab Planets

Timed-entry only, in peak-foliage season. Book the day tickets open (~90 days out) for your Nov 13 slot. Vegan Ramen UZU sits inside.

3 one-way tickets

✈ The three flights

HND→CTS · CTS→ICN (direct) · ICN→HND. LCC fares cheapest 2–3 months out. See Transport.

04

The jet lag, handled

São Paulo (UTC−3) → Tokyo (UTC+9) is a 12-hour eastward jump — the harder direction, because your body clock resists advancing. Unaided it can take ~10 days; with light + melatonin discipline you'll feel largely normal by Day 3. That's why the first three days are deliberately gentle.

Your #1 tool

☀️ Chase morning light

You'll wake at 4–5am the first days. Get outside within an hour of waking, before 10:00, every day for 4 days. After 20:00, dim everything.

0.5–1 mg

💉 Melatonin, low + early

Take 0.5–1 mg ~30 min before bedtime (22:00–23:00 JST), from arrival night, 3–5 nights. Never in daytime.

Cut-off 14:00

☕ Caffeine & meals

Eat on Tokyo time from landing. Coffee mornings only; hard stop at 2pm. No alcohol first 2–3 days. Hydrate hard.

NOV 10 · ARRIVAL
Land 16:15. No napping. Light dinner ~19:30, melatonin ~22:00, bed by 23:00.
NOV 11 · DAY 1
Wake ~4–5am — accept it. Morning light walk. Low-effort only (Meiji · Harajuku).
NOV 12 · DAY 2
Wake ~5–6am. Moderate day (Ginza). Afternoon dip; one 20-min nap max.
NOV 13 · DAY 3
Wake ~6–7am. Near-normal. teamLab + Toyosu. Keep the light ritual 1–2 more days.
NOV 14 · DAY 4
Full pace. Shibuya day. Essentially adjusted.
NOV 16–17
Disney lands on Day 6–7 on purpose — full energy for rope-drop.
DEC 4 · RETURN
Tokyo→Brazil is westward = easier (~3–5 days). Evening light at home, stay up late.

Getting around & the pass math

The headline: skip the Japan Rail Pass entirely. Because every long leg here is a flight, a nationwide pass (¥53,000+ pp on your Oct-2026 dates) can't recover more than ~¥14,000 of rail value. Same verdict for the JR Hokkaido pass.

🚄 Nationwide JR Pass

SKIP

¥53k / ¥84k / ¥105k (7/14/21-day, post-Oct-1-2026). You'd use ~¥14k of JR rail. Loses ~¥65k+ for two.

❄️ JR Hokkaido Pass

SKIP

5-day ¥22,000 pp. Your CTS↔Sapporo↔Asahikawa segments total only ~¥13,180 pp as singles (discounted on Ekinet).

📱 Mobile / Welcome Suica

BUY

Your everyday tap card — metro, JR, buses, konbini. Add Suica to Apple Wallet, or grab a physical Welcome Suica / Tourist PASMO at the airport.

🎫 Tokyo Subway 72h

HEAVY DAYS

¥2,000 covers all 13 metro/Toei lines, breaks even at ~8 rides. Buy 2–3 for dense days; Suica otherwise.
